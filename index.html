Plan smarter

Monsoon is cheapest

From June to September, a good AC dorm bed in Goa can cost around ₹350 to ₹500 a night.

Avoid the New Year rush

Between 20 December and 5 January, Goa rates often triple. Book 4 to 6 weeks ahead.

Pack for dorms

Bring two sturdy padlocks for under-bed lockers and keep valuables with you.

How a request works

  1. Pick a stay, your check-in date, nights and travellers.

Where our data comes from

Hotel prices and ratings come from public Expedia India listings. Hostel ranges come from travel guides and hostel comparison sites. Tourist numbers come from India’s tourism statistics. Rates move daily, so treat every price as a guide, not a quote.

Common questions

How do I book a stay?

Open Stays, tap Request booking on any hotel or hostel, and send your details. We confirm availability and the final price with you first.

Do I pay on this website?

No. Nothing is charged on this site. You pay only after we confirm the price with you.

Why are prices shown as “indicative”?

Hotels change rates by date and demand. The prices we show were listed on public sites, so the final price for your dates may be higher or lower.

Are taxes included?

Not always. Taxes may be extra, and we state the full amount when we confirm your request.

What does “typical range” mean for a hostel?

It is the usual dorm-bed range for that area and season, not a quote from that hostel.

My state is not in the stay list. What now?

Open States, pick your state and tap Request a quote. Tell us your dates and we will suggest options.
